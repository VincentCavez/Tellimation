SHIELD

Storytelling Helper for Interactive and Expressive Literacy Development

Vincent Cavez1, Marielle Zheng1, Momin Siddiqui2, Abbie L Olszewski3, Srirangaraj Setlur4, Maneesh Agrawala1, Hari Subramonyam1

1Stanford University 2Georgia Institute of Technology 3University of Nevada, Reno 4University of Buffalo

SHIELD helps children build their oral storytelling skills. The child looks at an illustrated scene and tells, out loud, what is happening. SHIELD listens, transcribes the story and compares it with the picture. When something is missing, unclear or could be said better (a character left out, a vague word, a grammar slip), the scene itself reacts with a short animation that draws the child's attention to it, so they can notice it and say it again in their own words, without ever being given the answer. At the end, you can download a readable analysis of the session.
